FlowLane / macOS

A small utility. Clear choices.

01 /

A route for each app

Ordered rules, with bundled helpers and XPC included by default.

02 /

Your proxies. Your Keychain.

Use your existing proxy service. Passwords are stored in macOS Keychain.

03 /

11 languages. Your choice.

Follow your Mac’s language or choose manually in Settings. Change text without resetting active connections.

Three steps. You decide when.

  1. 1

    Add apps

    Choose .app bundles first and assign proxies later. Unassigned rules stay disabled.

  2. 2

    Add proxy

    SOCKS5 and HTTP CONNECT are supported, with optional authentication.

  3. 3

    Start network routing

    Starting requires administrator approval. TUN receives traffic before routing it.

Stop before editing rules. Stopping disconnects connections handled by the core.

No Apple Developer account is required.
